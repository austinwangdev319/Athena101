# Athena101

Athena101 is a UCI-compatible chess-engine development project for local analysis, testing, and online play.
It was developed from a stockfish open-sourced code, intended to then evolve from that position to reach future in strength. 

Inspired by my own playing style, it prioritizes initiative over material, favors offbeat gambits, and seeks sharp tactical complications over conventional positional play. Its development mainly emphasizes on the exploration of how unconventional strategies and asymmetric positions can challenge established approaches to engine chess.

An ongoing experiment in chess AI, advanced pruning and time-depth tradeoff, and performance-driven optimization.
